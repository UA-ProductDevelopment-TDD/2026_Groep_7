3D-Printed Accessories For Petoi Bittle & Bittle X Robot Dogs: The Ultimate Guide
KAI MAI, JULY 6, 2025
Share
Unlock the full potential of your Petoi Bittle and Bittle X robot dogs with this comprehensive guide to 3D-printed accessories. Whether you’re an educator, hobbyist, student, robotics enthusiast, or developer, discover how to personalize, upgrade, and maximize your robot’s capabilities using the latest in DIY robotics, STEM technology, and open-source innovation.
Why 3D-Printed Accessories for Petoi Robots?


3D-printed robot accessories are transforming the way users interact with their programmable robot dogs and cats. Here’s why they matter:

Personalization: Give your robot a unique look and new abilities, from animal-themed heads to custom grippers.
STEM & Robotics Education: Make learning hands-on and interactive for students, teachers, and hobbyists.
Community Collaboration: Access and share open-source designs with a global Petoi community.
Easy Access: Download STL files and print at home, through online services, or at local maker spaces.
Expand Functionality: Add new features, sensors, and movement options to your robot dog

No 3D printer? You can use online 3D printing services or visit local maker spaces to bring your ideas to life.

A must-have for anyone assembling, programming, or demonstrating their robot dog. The Bittle stand provides a stable base, making it easier to work on your robot and ensuring accurate calibration.

Animal-Themed Add-Ons

Petoi Bittle dog is a small smart quadruped robot with a lot of character and distinct features. Don't get us wrong, we love the original form of our AI robot pets.  However, there are many tiny animal accessories that you can 3D-print to give your quadruped a new avatar to keep the fun going.

Beyond mere aesthetics, animal-themed accessories serve important educational and psychological functions in robotics education:

While the main body of the robot will remain the same, these small accessories are easy to put on and will transform your pets into various creatures for you to interact, play, program, and learn with!

Beetle Horn


Replace the dog face with a horn for object-pushing and playful robot battles.  The horn can be used like an arm to push objects and to play friendly battles between Bittles.

As you can see in the video above, the 3D printed Beetle horn is one of the most popular accessories to pair with a Bittle Robot Dog.

Adds a unique look and interactive play value.
Introduces concepts of biomimicry in engineering design.
Supports competitive robotics activities and challenges.  The competition can be a Sumo-style game match each Bittle is trying to push others out of the circle.
Introduce a fantastic way to learn and explore Petoi Bittle movement, spatial awareness, and robot mechanics!
Great for creative classroom activities and competitions.
Fox head

Swap the head for a fox design to give your robot a new character.

Inspires storytelling and imaginative play.
Design and program some new fox moves.
Easy to install and remove for quick customization.
Bone for Petoi Bittle dog

Every dog loves to clamp a bone in its mouth.  So does Bittle robot dog. 


Print a bone for Bittle to “hold” in its mouth for playful interactions.

Perfect for photos, demos, and engaging students.
Quick introduction to 3D modeling and printing for beginners
A simple accessory that adds charm to your robot dog.

Enables attachment of Lego bricks for custom builds and cargo boxes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Question: Do I need a 3D printer to use these accessories?


Answer: No. Multiple options exist, including:

Online 3D printing services like Shapeways or Printables
Local maker spaces and libraries with 3D printing facilities
Educational institution partnerships for shared resources
Community workshops and fab labs
Question:  Are accessories compatible with both Bittle and Bittle X?

Answer: Yes, the open-source design philosophy ensures broad compatibility across Petoi platforms. Most accessories work seamlessly with both models, though some advanced features may require specific hardware configurations.

Question: Where can I find STL files and design resources?

Answer: All the STL files an resources can be found on the external links we share in each accessory section.

Question: Can students design their own accessories?

Absolutely! Original design development is a core educational objective:

Students can use CAD software like Tinkercad, Fusion 360, or Blender
Collaborative design projects encourage peer learning
Contributing to the global robotics community builds professional skills
Design thinking methodologies support systematic problem-solving
Question:  What materials are recommended for educational environments?


Material selection depends on application and safety requirements:

PLA (Polylactic Acid): Ideal for most educational applications due to safety and ease of use
PETG: Excellent balance of strength and flexibility
ABS: Better for parts requiring durability and heat resistance
